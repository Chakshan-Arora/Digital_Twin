================================================================================
HUMAN DIGITAL TWIN (HDT) - FRONTEND IMPLEMENTATION & CHANGES LOG
================================================================================
DISCLAIMER: RESEARCH PROTOTYPE ONLY - NOT A CLINICAL TOOL
This software is an experimental research prototype for uncertainty-aware 3D
clearance analysis on synthetic and phantom medical scans. It is strictly not
intended for clinical use, diagnosis, treatment planning, surgical navigation,
safety assurance, or surgical success prediction.
================================================================================

1. TECH STACK & ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Framework: React 19 + TypeScript (Strict typing, ESM, verbatimModuleSyntax)
- Build Tool: Vite 8 (Hot Module Replacement, fast production builds)
- 3D Mesh Viewer: Three.js (v0.186.x) with OBJLoader, OrbitControls, Custom Shaders
- 2D MPR Viewer: @niivue/niivue (Axial, Coronal, Sagittal orthogonal slicing)
- Data Visualization: Chart.js + react-chartjs-2 (Monte Carlo histograms)
- Icons: lucide-react
- Coordinate Convention: Physical RAS (Right, Anterior, Superior) in millimetres (mm)


2. IMPLEMENTED WORKSTATIONS & MODULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. Application Shell & State Router
   - frontend/src/App.tsx:
     Central Application Shell. Manages top-level active workstation mode
     ('clearance' vs 'explorer') synchronized with window.location.hash
     (#clearance / #explorer). Manages backend connection health checks,
     re-triggers Three.js view recalculations on mode changes, and renders
     the shared header and disclaimer footer.
   - frontend/src/components/Header.tsx:
     Application top bar with real-time FastAPI backend health status pill,
     active case / avatar model indicator, non-clinical research prototype
     disclaimer badge, and a segmented mode toggle pill:
     [ 🔬 Clearance Lab | 🧍 Body Explorer ].

B. Clearance Lab Workstation (Encapsulated)
   - frontend/src/layout/ClearanceLabWorkstation.tsx:
     Dedicated workstation for surgical clearance and uncertainty analysis.
     Preserves 100% of the original Clearance Lab functionality, state,
     and tests untouched.
   - frontend/src/components/CaseDetailsCard.tsx:
     Displays selected case information, physical origin (ox, oy, oz), voxel
     spacing (sz, sy, sx) in mm, volume dimensions, and 3x3 direction cosine
     matrix in RAS space. Lists target and risk structures with direct NIfTI downloads.
   - frontend/src/components/SliceViewer.tsx:
     Renders multi-planar reconstruction (MPR) orthogonal 2D slice views of the
     primary NIfTI scan volume with colorized binary structure masks.
   - frontend/src/components/Viewer3D.tsx:
     Loads authoritative surface polygon meshes (.obj) exported by backend marching
     cubes in physical RAS mm. Shows 3D closest-points line vector and synchronized
     crosshair sphere.
   - frontend/src/components/ClearancePanel.tsx:
     Triggers signed face-to-face Euclidean distance, closest-point coordinates,
     and surface distance stats (min, mean, max, HD95).
   - frontend/src/components/UncertaintyPanel.tsx & HistogramChart.tsx:
     Configures Gaussian random field parameters, executes Monte Carlo simulation,
     and charts collision probability P(clearance < threshold).

C. Body Explorer: Hologram Hull, Tumor Margin Shell & In-Situ Cut-Plane
   - frontend/src/layout/BodyExplorerWorkstation.tsx:
     Full-body digital twin workstation with internal pathology visualization:
     • Translucent 'Glass / Cyber' Holographic Body Envelope:
       Custom Fresnel rim-lighting shader (hologramShaders.ts) keeps body center
       transparent while outlining silhouettes, completely eliminating depth-sorting bugs.
     • Cranial Tumor Mesh Integration:
       Loads authoritative marching cubes surface mesh (/tumor_mesh.obj) positioned
       anatomically inside the cranial vault.
     • In-Situ 2D MRI Radiology Cut-Plane:
       2D planar slice strictly pinned at the tumor's center point in physical space.
       Features Tri-Planar orientation toggles:
       - Axial (Transverse horizontal plane)
       - Coronal (Front-to-back frontal plane)
       - Sagittal (Side-to-side plane)
       Includes an opacity slider (20% to 100%) and authentic procedural MRI texture
       (cortex, ventricles, sulci, and contrast-enhancing tumor hyperintensity).
     • Adjacent Critical Risk Structure:
       3D tube geometry representing carotid/cranial vascular risk structure in Sky Blue.
     • Closest-Point Clearance Vector:
       Displays 3D distance line vector and endpoint spheres connecting tumor to risk structure.
     • Interactive Surgical Margin Expansion Shell:
       Real-time slider (0.0 to 10.0 mm) expanding tumor vertices along surface normals.
       Features live collision alert (Safe Clearance vs. Margin Compromised).
     • Camera Focal Controls:
       One-click toggle between [Whole-Body Macro View] and [Zoom to Cranial Tumor].
     • Preset selection (Standard, Tall, Compact) and stature slider (1400–2100 mm).
   - frontend/src/layout/mriSliceTexture.ts & mriSliceTexture.test.ts:
     Procedural generator for high-resolution medical MRI slice textures across
     Axial, Coronal, and Sagittal cross-sections with SRGBColorSpace.
   - frontend/src/layout/hologramShaders.ts:
     Shader materials: createHologramFresnelMaterial (Fresnel rim glow) and
     createMarginShellMaterial (translucent amber surgical margin expansion halo).
   - frontend/src/layout/presets.ts & presets.test.ts:
     Anatomical body models with scaleBodyParams() maintaining floor plane at Y=0
     and bilateral symmetry. Tested with 100% pass rate.
